# Orion Mobile v4.0.0 — Discover Stories Through a Clip

Discover a scene. Find your next story. Stay connected to Orion.

Orion 4.0 brings **Glimpse**, the **Orion Feedback Center**, a one-time Welcome and native Release Notes to Mobile, alongside better Anime catalog matching. Your familiar Orion remains at the heart of the experience.

This is Orion's **final major feature release**. Bug fixes, compatibility work, essential maintenance and improvements driven by feedback may still follow where necessary.

---

## What's New in Orion Mobile v4.0.0

### 📱 Meet Glimpse

- **Discover movies, series and anime through clips**, with more reels loading as you continue scrolling.
- Find official clips and a maintained selection of scenes from additional publishers. Each reel connects to its matching Orion title through **Movie details, Series details or Anime details**.
- Keep **private Likes on this device**. The Liked tab gives you sorting and a stable place in your collection; new Likes follow your chosen order.
- **Search for a title**, then scroll its matching clips. Media, genre and region filters help refine the feed, and selected filters show what you chose.
- Enjoy a responsive Stage that respects the source picture's dimensions without blindly cropping it to fill the screen.
- Use the fixed Previous and Next controls, or swipe to move through reels. Only the settled reel owns active playback.
- Choose **Start muted, Auto-advance, Ambient glow and Remember my place** in Settings → Glimpse. Auto-advance moves on when the clip ends.
- Ambient presentation follows the playing clip's colors where available, with artwork as a fallback. **Efficiency and Reduced Motion** keep the experience restrained.
- Offline messaging and reconnection make it clear when streaming is unavailable, while private Likes remain saved locally.

### 💬 Your Voice Has a Place in Orion

- Open the new **Feedback Center** from the sidebar footer to **Report a Problem, Suggest a Feature or share General Feedback**.
- Choose a problem area and severity. Orion can include safe technical diagnostics so you do not need to collect technical information yourself.
- Review the included information and choose whether to add an optional screenshot or reply address. Nothing is sent until you choose Send.
- Receive an **Orion Feedback ID** after a successful submission. Copy it, find the report in local **Feedback History**, or add more information later.
- An interrupted or offline send keeps your draft available for a manual retry.
- A compact shared status and Feedback footer keeps these utilities within reach without adding another main navigation destination.

### ✨ Welcome to Orion 4.0

- Existing users receive a **one-time Welcome after updating**, introducing Glimpse and the Feedback Center.
- Choose **Explore Glimpse** or **Continue to Orion**. Your deliberate dismissal is remembered.
- The Welcome works offline and respects your motion and Performance Profile preferences. Fresh installations keep their own first-run experience.

### 📖 Release Notes That Feel Like Orion

- Settings → Update now opens **View Release Notes** in a dedicated native Orion dialog.
- Notes scroll independently while the release heading and Close action remain within reach. Android Back closes the dialog.
- Read the installed notes **offline**, with theme-aware presentation and support for larger text and accessibility.
- Opening or closing notes preserves the existing Update state and does not start another update check.

### 🎞️ Better Anime Catalog Matching

- Anime films and series use **Orion's canonical catalog identity**, with dynamic verification of the matching Anime entry.
- Seasons, sequels and split cours retain their logical Orion episode numbering while providers receive the verified episode they require.
- New Anime playback prioritizes a dedicated Anime source after verification. A saved or deliberately chosen General source remains respected.
- Movie and series source affinity stay separate, preserving the appropriate provider and **Sub/Dub** choice across restart.
- Ambiguous mappings and unreleased episodes are kept out of playback rather than guessed.

## Still Orion

Home, Movies, TV, Anime, Discover and Search, Media Details, your Library, Continue Watching, My List, downloads and offline playback remain part of Orion. Account, Google sign-in, Orion Cloud, notifications, Home Layout, themes, Performance Profiles, Reduced Motion and Orion Connect remain available.

Existing playback controls, source selection, Resume, Start from Beginning, Replay Last 30, Next Episode and embedded-player protection remain the foundation of the Mobile experience.

## Source Availability

Clips depend on the uploader's availability, regional permissions and support for embedded playback. When an upload cannot play in Orion, use the available recovery actions or continue to another reel.

AniLink remains a manual Anime source. Some sources may fail to start or miss the incoming saved position; **Retry or another source may be needed**. Orion keeps source capabilities visible and does not promote an unreliable source into automatic playback.

## 🔐 Release & Update Integrity

The Android app keeps its permanent **com.okali.orion** package and Orion signing authority. Install the official signed APK as an in-place update to retain existing app data; do not uninstall Orion first.

Orion's existing updater verifies release identity, integrity and signer before installation. A mismatched or unverified package is rejected.
