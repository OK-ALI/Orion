# Orion v3.0.0 — A Multiverse of Stories (Stable Production Release)

> "A universe made to be felt."

Orion v3.0.0 marks the definitive milestone release of the Orion entertainment platform, uniting **Orion Desktop** and the all-new **Orion Mobile** into a seamless, interconnected ecosystem. Whether you are enjoying high-fidelity Cinema, experiencing reactive audio inside Music Planet, or relaxing on your couch controlling your PC via Orion Connect, v3.0 delivers a cohesive multiverse experience.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Updating from Orion v3.0.0 to newer releases:**
> If you are on Orion v3.0.0 and encounter an automatic installation notice stating *"Orion could not verify this update"* after downloading:
> - **Why this happens**: Orion v3.0.0 applies strict cryptographic security checks on all incoming updates. On some Windows systems without our preview signing authority pre-configured, automatic launching is safely paused as a precaution.
> - **One-Time Easy Update**: Simply download and run the latest installer directly from our [Releases page](https://github.com/OK-ALI/Orion/releases). Your watchlist, settings, and media library will remain 100% intact, and all future updates from v3.1.0 onward will verify and install automatically in a single click!

## 1. Orion Desktop — The Cinematic Powerhouse

### 🎬 Orion Cinema
- **Unified Streaming & Multi-Provider Engine**: Stream movies, series, and anime with intelligent multi-source fallback. If a provider is degraded, Orion effortlessly falls back to active alternative sources.
- **Sandboxed Ad-Blocking Webview**: Proprietary DOM cleaning and network-level host interception that neutralizes click-hijacks, popup overlays, and intrusive trackers before they render.
- **Continuous Progress & Episode Auto-Advance**: Seamlessly tracks your watch history, in-progress checkpoints, and automatically queues the next episode with countdown overlays.
- **Smart Subtitles Engine**: Integrated SubDL and OpenSubtitles search with automatic title matching, millisecond-accurate sync offset tuning (`-` / `+`), and readable typography.
- **Constellation Taste Map**: An interactive visual node graph visualizing your saved movies, directors, actors, and genre connections, creating a personalized cinematic universe.

### 🎵 Orion Music Planet
- **Rich Streaming Catalog**: Access millions of songs and albums powered by YouTube Music, with instant audio resolution.
- **Synchronized Lyrics**: Real-time scrolling lyrics powered by LRCLib.
- **Living Eclipse Scene & Audio-Reactive Orb**: A real-time audio visualizer featuring an organic reactive Orb, semantic themes, and accessible reduced-motion support.
- **Persistent Dock & Queue**: Full queue management, shuffle, repeat, listening history, favorites, and custom playlist folders.
- **Local Library Offline Audio**: Index and play your local MP3, FLAC, and AAC audio files directly with full visualizer support.

### 🖼️ Popout Player (Picture-in-Picture)
- **Always-on-Top Floating Mini-Player**: Detach any movie, show, or music stream into a compact, resizable floating window.
- **Zero-Interruption Handoff**: Smooth transitions between full player and mini-player while preserving exact playback position and audio state.

### 💾 Downloads & Resilient Offline Playback
- **Multi-Threaded Chunk Downloader**: Download movies, episodes, and media for offline viewing with pause and resume support.
- **Battery & Network Guard**: Automatically pauses downloads on critical battery or network drop, with an intelligent recovery flow when power or connectivity returns.
- **Local File Player**: Direct drag-and-drop or file browsing for local video (MKV, MP4, WebM) with hardware-accelerated rendering.

### ☁️ Cloud Sync & Privacy
- **Google Drive Backup**: Back up your watchlist, playlists, playback history, and preferences to your private Google Drive via bundled OAuth PKCE (no Cloud Console setup required).
- **Zero Telemetry Leakage**: Local paths, signed URLs, credentials, and playback sessions remain 100% private to your device.

## 3. Orion Connect — The Flagship Companion & Remote Experience

Orion Connect bridges your mobile device and desktop over your local Wi-Fi network with ultra-low latency:

### 🔒 Enterprise-Grade Local Security
- **Zero-Configuration Discovery**: Automatic local network discovery via mDNS (`_orion-connect._tcp`).
- **Encrypted WebSocket Transport**: Secured with local TLS and trusted cryptographic fingerprinting.
- **Ephemeral 6-Digit PIN**: Secure rotating PIN authentication with brute-force lockout protection (Pairing Guard).
- **Multi-Device Support**: Pair multiple phones; explicit Single-Controller Ownership ensures clean command execution with instant "Take Control" handover.

### 🎮 Comprehensive Remote Control
- **Full Media Transport**: Play, Pause, Rewind 10s, Fast-Forward 10s, Previous Track, Next Track, and Stop.
- **Live Continuous Scrubber**: High-resolution scrub bar with local finger tracking and intelligent post-seek reconciliation that eliminates thumb bounce and rubber-banding.
- **Real-Time Telemetry**: Live display of playback elapsed time, remaining duration, buffering status, media artwork, title, and episode info.
- **Context Switching**: Remotely switch between Main Player, Fullscreen, Popout Mini-Player, or return to Desktop Home with a single tap.

### 💻 First-Class PC Hardware Controls
- **Windows Master Volume**: Real-time volume slider (0–100%) and step buttons that adjust your PC's master audio.
- **Two-Way Hardware Volume Sync**: If volume is adjusted physically on your PC (via keyboard volume keys or the Windows taskbar), Orion Connect updates on your phone in real time.
- **Master Mute Toggle**: Instant hardware mute/unmute of your computer.
- **Display Brightness Control**: Adjust your monitor's hardware backlight directly from your couch:
  - Supports **Laptops & All-in-Ones** via Windows ACPI WMI.
  - Supports **External Desktop Monitors** via VESA DDC/CI Win32 Physical Monitor APIs (`dxva2.dll`).

### 🖱️ Bi-Directional Remote Pointer, Touchpad & Scroll Strip
- **Trackpad Mode**: Smooth relative cursor navigation with realistic inertia, tap-to-select, and two-finger vertical scrolling.
- **Vertical Scroll Slider Strip**: A dedicated 44px vertical scroll strip beside the mobile trackpad with tactile chevrons and center grip for effortless single-thumb desktop scrolling.
- **Direct 1:1 Surface Mode**: Maps your phone screen directly to your desktop player window for direct, pinpoint interaction.
- **Surface-Aware Webview Routing**: Interact directly with embedded provider controls, startup play buttons, and subtitle menus without leaving your couch.
