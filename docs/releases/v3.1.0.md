# Orion v3.1.0 — Seamless Living-Room Entertainment & Battery Performance

> "Effortless entertainment, everywhere you watch."

Orion v3.1.0 brings major enhancements to your everyday streaming experience, focusing on seamless laptop mobility, ultra-fast remote typing, intuitive carousel browsing from your phone, and a refreshed companion interface.

---

## What's New in Orion v3.1.0

### 🔋 Seamless Laptop Streaming on Battery Power
- **Zero-Stall Playback When Unplugged**: Enjoy uninterrupted movies, series, and music streams when taking your laptop off the charger. Orion now automatically coordinates with your operating system's power management to eliminate sudden buffering pauses, loading spinners, and network latency drops on battery.
- **Intelligent Power Balancing**: Keeps video decoding running smoothly at full performance while intelligently conserving energy during background tasks.
- **Adaptive Ambient Lighting**: Ambient lighting automatically scales its sampling on battery power, keeping your laptop running cooler and maximizing battery life during movie marathons.
- **Dedicated Video Bandwidth**: Automatically prioritizes video streams over background sync tasks on battery so your playback receives maximum Wi-Fi bandwidth.

### ⚡ Ultra-Responsive Live Remote Typing
- **Instant Search Synchronization**: Type movie titles, actor names, music tracks, and settings directly from your phone's keyboard into your desktop in real time.
- **Live Interactive Results**: Watch search results populate dynamically on your computer screen as you type on mobile, without needing to press Enter or tap Send.
- **Universal Input Detection**: Works across Cinema Search, Music Planet, Constellation Taste Map, Settings, and modal input fields.
- **Quick-Clear Controls**: Easily clear text with a single tap on the inline mobile input button.

### ↔️ Effortless Carousel & Slider Horizontal Navigation
- **Dedicated Horizontal Scroll Strip**: Added a tactile horizontal scroll strip directly beneath the mobile touchpad, allowing you to glide smoothly across movie shelves, season episodes, and music playlists with a single thumb.
- **Natural Touchpad Swipes**: Swipe across the mobile trackpad or use two fingers to pan horizontally through wide catalog rows and media collections.
- **Intuitive Spatial Navigation**: Seamlessly steps focus across cards and buttons on your desktop screen when exploring media collections.

### ✨ Redesigned Desktop Smart Connect Interface
- **Modern Glassmorphic Visuals**: Replaced rigid legacy dialogs with a sleek, translucent modal that automatically matches your active Orion color theme.
- **Clear Device Status Indicators**: View connected phones, active controller permissions, and connection health with crisp, accessible status indicators.
- **Frictionless Disconnect & Handoff**: Cleanly switch controllers or disconnect with a single click.

### 🔊 Tactile Sound Feedback
- **Audio Connection Cues**: Subtle, pleasant sound effects confirm when your mobile companion pairs successfully or disconnects, giving you instant auditory confirmation from across the room.

### 🎮 TV & Accessibility D-Pad Navigation
- **Dedicated Directional Pad**: Secondary directional controls are now clearly organized under "TV & Accessibility D-Pad", providing straightforward step-by-step navigation tailored for couch viewing and big-screen setups.
